Design System

Color pallet

My main color will be oxford blue. This has a Contrast Ratio of 16.45:1.

My Secondary color will be Cerulean. This has a Contrast Ratio of 4.79:1

My Neutral color will be Magenta. This has a Contrast Ratio of 7.72:1

Fonts

The fonts I will use for body and small text will be Arial.

The font I will use for headers will be Times New Roman.

Spacing

I will use 8px for a margin, 16 for padding, 32 for gaps between divs.

Reflection:

Identity and Culture - The color choices reflect my identity on a simple basis, blue is my favorite color, but also because the lighter tones reflect my softer identity. I am a very non-confrontational, empathetic person, and I wanted my font and color choices to reflect that. The lighter isual elements feel the most authnticto me, as I believe the point of a webpage is to be gentle and assessable, not harsh.

Assessability Considerations - I have recently decided to focus on Assessability in my web pages more than I was. This has had the side effect of forcing me to make more thoughtful decisions about the structure of my web pages. This has unfortunetly led to more stress and time in my web page than I would have liked, but that is an acceptable price to pay. An example would be conserns over colors and readability, as well as web Assessability for buttons.

Design Process - Some things that supprised me about creating a design system were just how hard color matching is, and just how prevalent spacing is. The color matching needed to be consistent for all the colors I chose, which led to more time and effort in choosing the colors. I had a primary color in mind from the beginning, but it was difficult to find secondary colors. For buttons, I found it actually an interesting vhallenge, as button interaction is a intersting use of CSS. I am a fan of creative CSS use and I enjoy working with fun and interesing styles. Button interaction just seemed like the natural extension.

Future Applications - I might change this formula in the future to better suit different webpages primarily by changing the layout of the page. I believe changing the layout of div's to better suit the style of webpage would be an interesting change. In that way I believe the overall structure is the most flexible out of all the elements of the page. I might also change the sizes of the text and add it pictures and hyperlinks, depending on what kind of page it will be. A page with fewer text will need images and smaller paragraphs. The element that I'm least willing to change is the color scheme. I'm rather attacked to the color's I came up with, as I think they match each other well. Above all I am unwilling to change my main color, as it is important to my life and I'm very attacked to it.
